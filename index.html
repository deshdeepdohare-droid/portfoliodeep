Frequent
Interrogations.

Transparency is the bedrock of my client relationships. Here are detailed answers to common technical and procedural queries.

What is the 'Deep' methodology in practice?

It's a synthesis of atomic design and performance engineering. We build components that are small, reusable, and optimized for minimal memory overhead. I utilize TypeScript strictly to ensure that the code is self-documenting and virtually bug-free from the start.

How do you handle technical debt?

By preventing it. I favor "Boring Technology" where reliability is proven, combined with modern architectural patterns. Every commit is reviewed against strict performance and security benchmarks. We build for the next five years, not the next five days.

Why choose a developer based in Gwalior?

Because it allows me to offer world-class engineering quality with a perspective of architectural precision inherited from my surroundings. I operate with lower overhead than Silicon Valley agencies while maintaining higher technical standards through focused, deep-work sessions.

Do you offer white-label development?

Yes. I frequently partner with creative agencies as their secret technical powerhouse. I can work within your project management tools (Jira, Linear, Asana) and maintain complete anonymity while delivering top-tier code for your clients.

What is your project management style?

I utilize a "Two-Week Sprint" model. You receive a Loom video update every Friday, and we have a strategic sync call every alternate Tuesday. All progress is tracked in a shared Linear workspace for absolute visibility.
